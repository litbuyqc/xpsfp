03 / Oopbuy shopping guide

Make every comparison useful.

Good Oopbuy spreadsheet finds begin with clear requirements. Decide what you need before comparing product listings.

A useful shortlist records evidence: the chosen variant, actual measurements, material information and the date you checked the listing.

Start browsing CAFANS ↗

  1. Choose a category and a purpose

    Start with daily sneakers, a lightweight layer or a specific accessory. For an Oopbuy shoes spreadsheet, distinguish casual styling from performance requirements.

  2. Check measurements before size labels

    For an Oopbuy clothing spreadsheet, record chest width, body length, waist and inseam where relevant. Compare measurements with a garment that already fits.

  3. Inspect the selected variant

    Confirm that photos, color, material and specifications describe the exact option you intend to order. Ask for missing details instead of filling gaps with assumptions.

  4. Estimate the complete cost

    Keep the item price separate from shipping, service charges and applicable import costs. Compare totals in one currency and record when each estimate was obtained.

04 / The comparison desk

What should your shortlist include?

Use these fields when building an Oopbuy product list or comparing items across categories.

Practical fields for a shopping comparison spreadsheet
Category Record these details Check before deciding
Shoes Model, selected size, measurement method, upper and sole materials Does the listing explain its sizing convention and intended use?
Tops & jackets Chest width, length, shoulder width, sleeve length and composition Are the measurements compatible with your preferred fit and layering?
Pants & shorts Waist, rise, inseam, leg opening and stretch information Is the waist measurement relaxed, stretched or a body measurement?
Headwear & sets Head circumference or adjustment range; separate measurements for each set piece Are all included pieces and their sizes clearly specified?
Accessories Dimensions, weight if provided, material, finish and closure type Do the measurements and materials suit the intended use?
Every item Product URL, variant, listed price, date checked and estimated total cost Have availability, seller terms and any unverified claims been checked?

Keep observation separate from verification. A product photo can show visible details. It cannot, by itself, establish authenticity, durability, stock availability or final delivery cost.

05 / Quick answers

Before you follow a product link.

What is an Oopbuy spreadsheet?

An Oopbuy spreadsheet is a product discovery list used to organize shopping links and comparison notes. Its contents depend on the person maintaining it. A listing alone does not verify product quality, authenticity, availability or compatibility with a shopping service.

Does this page contain a downloadable spreadsheet?

No. This page provides a category directory and shopping guidance. Its shopping links open CAFANS pages; it does not provide a downloadable spreadsheet or a live Oopbuy inventory feed.

How should I compare shoe sizes?

Use the individual listing's size chart and measurement instructions. Compare foot length or insole length only when the seller specifies which measurement is used. Do not assume that the same size label fits identically across models.

What belongs in a product comparison spreadsheet?

Record the product URL, selected color, size, material, listed price, measurement notes and the date checked. Add shipping estimates and other applicable charges separately so that the total cost remains clear.

Are branded products authenticated by this guide?

No. Brand references and editorial images do not authenticate products or establish an authorized seller relationship. Check provenance, seller information and the specific listing before purchasing.

Where do the shopping links lead?

All shopping links lead to CAFANS. The ten category tiles open their corresponding cafans.com category pages, while general browsing buttons open https://www.cafans.com.
